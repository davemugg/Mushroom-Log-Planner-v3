📊 Tile Size Study

Which repeating tile, up to 7×7, earns the most gold per square of land? Results from 1,440 runs of the planner's optimizer.

← Planner📖 Guide

A repeating tile is a small pattern of logs, trees and walkways that you copy side by side as your farm grows. The planner's optimizer was run many times for every tile size from 2×2 to 7×7. Each result was scored by its gold per square of land per day, with copies of the tile on every side, so tiles of different sizes compare fairly. Two walkway rules were tested:

  • Default walkways: walkways must run off the tile and join the next copy in at least one direction.
  • One connected network: walkways must join up both across and down, so every tile connects to every other.
